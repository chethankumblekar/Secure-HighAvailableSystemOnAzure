What this is

Two layers, shipped together

Primary deliverable

The Platform

A self-service, golden-path system for standing up secure, observable, cost-governed multi-tenant services on Azure. This is the operational surface a platform team owns — Terraform IaC, GitOps delivery, supply-chain security, observability, an internal developer platform, and FinOps governance.

Proof, not the point

The Reference Workload

One deliberately simple multi-tenant API service that exists only to prove the platform works end-to-end. Kept simple on purpose — the platform is what's under evaluation here, not the workload's business logic.

System flow

From a laptop to two clouds

Three tiers, cost-gated in that order: unlimited local iteration, free CI on GitHub, and real cloud infrastructure only in short, deliberate bursts.
